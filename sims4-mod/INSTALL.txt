VOXTA BRIDGE FOR THE SIMS 4
===========================

1. Put this whole "Voxta" folder into your Mods folder:
   Documents\Electronic Arts\The Sims 4\Mods\Voxta\
   (voxta_bridge.ts4script must be no deeper than one folder inside Mods.)

2. In the game: Options -> Game Options -> Other ->
   tick "Enable Custom Content and Mods" and "Script Mods Allowed", then restart the game.

3. In Voxta Server: add the "The Sims 4" module, and turn on the "sims4" augmentation
   for a character whose name matches one of your Sims (e.g. "Bella Goth" or just "Bella").

4. Load your household. The mod connects to Voxta by itself.

Cheat console (Ctrl+Shift+C):
   voxta.status     connection and household check
   voxta.test       shows a test notification
   voxta.reconnect  try connecting again now
   voxta.dump       writes what the mod sees to voxta_bridge.log

Problems? Look at voxta_bridge.log in this folder.
